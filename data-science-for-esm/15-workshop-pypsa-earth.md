# Introduction to PyPSA-Earth

[PyPSA-Earth](https://github.com/pypsa-meets-earth/pypsa-earth) is an open-source global energy system model built on top of [PyPSA](https://pypsa.org). It enables the modelling and optimisation of energy systems for any region in the world, making it particularly valuable for studies in emerging economies and for global energy transition scenarios.

This section provides a video-based introduction to PyPSA-Earth, covering the basics of the model, setup instructions, debugging strategies, and result analysis.
